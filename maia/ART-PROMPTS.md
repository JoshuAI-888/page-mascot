# Māia 02 sprite generation specifications

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool, transparent_background=true. No CLI/API fallback. The approved identity sheet was the primary reference for all three generations; the generated directions sheet was additionally supplied for reactions and actions to maintain alignment.

## Shared identity constraints

Identity-preserve. Nine evenly spaced full-body renders in a square transparent 3×3 atlas. Exact approved Māia 02 kea, realistic stylized 3D layered olive/bronze feathers, short bronze crest, moderate head/body proportions, amber eyes, compact downward-hooked graphite beak, orange underwing accents. Neatly knotted two-tone orange scarf, pale orange “Māia” printed bottom-to-top with M closest to lower tip and macron over first a. No mirroring, human mouth, teeth, hands, extra limbs, tall crest or oversized eyes. Frontal body, consistent camera, scale, lighting and grounded-foot baseline. Wide margins; no labels, gridlines, background or external shadows. Only scarf text permitted. Requested occupancy 76% of cell height; generated occupancy is greater, and the returned atlas is 1,254 px square.

## Directions

Same neutral warm expression and body stance in every cell. Only eyes/head angle change subtly, requested maximum 12 degrees. Row 1: viewer upper-left, up, upper-right. Row 2: viewer left, directly at viewer, viewer right. Row 3: lower-left, down, lower-right. No symbols. Review result: major identity retained; diagonal direction is subtle, bottom-right needs artwork correction. Do not mark this as an approved direction set.

## Reactions

Same frontal body. Row 1: (1) naturally closed eyelids, (2) warm affectionate eyes with tiny orange heart, (3) bright excited eyes with three small gold sparkles. Row 2: (4) mild surprise with lower mandible slightly open, (5) one-eye wink, (6) bashful lowered lids and very small head tilt. Row 3: (7) sleepy/bored half-lidded eyes, (8) unfocused squint with two small orbital arcs, (9) delighted happy eyes and slightly open lower mandible. Preserve upper beak and skull; no human grin. Review result: expression changes visible, but feather/scarf/beak details vary; candidate only.

## Actions

Row 1: left avian foot lifts toward cheek while right supports body; toe claws contact cheek; foot scratches lower on cheek. Exactly two avian feet total, wings tucked, small head inclination. Row 2: wings slightly outward; higher lift revealing orange underwings; folding toward rest, no jump and no clipping. Row 3: neutral; warm smiling eye squint and minimal lower-mandible opening; settled happy eyes. No floating symbols. Review result: actual cheek contact and distinct wing poses, but stepped motion and some body shifts; not a continuous rig.
